Question types

WASD walk · mouse: look · Space jump · Shift run · E or click: use · Esc pause
On a phone or tablet: move with the left stick, drag to look, and tap USE.
Each level has 5 places with 3 locks in each place: 15 Latin questions in all.
Latin sentences from Prep School Latin by R. C. Bass, used under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike licence.

HOW TO OPEN THE WAY

  1. Look for golden sparkles. Each one marks a lock.
  2. Walk up to the lock and press E. On a tablet, tap USE.
  3. Answer the Latin question to open the lock.
  4. Open all three locks in a place, and the door to the next place opens.
