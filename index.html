HOW TO OPEN THE WAY

  1. Each place has three locks. The box in the top corner tells you what to look for.
  2. ✦ Golden sparkles mark a locked box or jar. 🗣 People with sparkles over their heads will set you a question. ▣ Gold-edged floor tiles open when you stand on them. 📜 Hidden scrolls do not sparkle at all, so search carefully.
  3. Walk up and press E (on a tablet, tap USE), then answer the Latin question.
  4. Open all three, and the door to the next place opens. Every place also hides secrets. Explore!
